Why windows get so dirty here

  • Our water is really hard.San Antonio tap water runs 15 to 20 grains per gallon. When the sprinklers hit the glass it dries into white spots that regular cleaner just smears.
  • Cedar in winter, oak in spring.Pollen settles on the glass and bakes into a yellow film.
  • Dust and heat.Construction dust and summer heat cook grime onto screens and tracks. Clean screens are half of what makes a window look clear.
